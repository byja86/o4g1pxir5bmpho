5Wandile Sihlobo: Wheat plantings hit lowest level since 1929

Business Day · Wandile Sihlobo · 4 Aug · ~5 min read
The headline sounds like a crisis, but Sihlobo's read is structural, not a failure: ample global wheat at lower prices plus higher input costs made planting more wheat simply uneconomical for SA farmers this season. That's the difference between reacting to a scary number and actually understanding SA's agricultural economics.

6Rand holds near 5-month peak after US inflation data

CNBC Africa · 12 Aug · ~4 min read
The rand's strength has almost nothing to do with SA right now — it's softer US inflation cooling Fed rate-hike bets and lifting emerging-market currencies broadly. Good discipline for reading any SA market move: separate what actually happened locally from what just happened to land in SA's favour.
