Theme of the day: Every item today is about what sits underneath a reported figure. Clyde Rossouw at Ninety One makes the sharpest version of it: the gap between the capital going into AI, the cash flows that capital actually generates and the earnings being reported is, in his words, close to unparalleled in market history — and semiconductor names that were losing money in 2022 are now worth more than a trillion dollars because everybody drew the same conclusion at the same time. PEX found the same instinct inside finance teams: 66% want AI, only 28% will let it decide anything, and the top barrier named by 36% is simply not trusting the output.
