Theme of the day: Money got more expensive everywhere at once, and today’s brief follows that single fact through four different rooms. Global sovereign bond yields hit their highest level since 2008 and South Africa’s 10-year has drifted up with them, which means Pretoria pays more even if Pretoria does nothing wrong. In private equity, HarbourVest’s data shows the software repricing has finally reached portfolio marks and pushed global buyout returns negative in Q1 — the same discount-rate arithmetic, arriving on a lag. And in the US, only 35% of finance leaders can confidently measure the return on their AI spend, which is the same problem in miniature — if capital costs more, you had better be able to prove what it earns.
