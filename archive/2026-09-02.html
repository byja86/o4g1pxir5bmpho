Theme of the day: Buying the thing is the easy part. A University of Glasgow study of AI investment, written up as today’s ACCA CPD article, found no reliable link between how much a company spends on AI and how efficient it becomes — not even five years out. What separated the winners was everything around the spend: competitive pressure, management capability, patient owners and long-dated funding. The same shape runs through the rest of today. A startup CFO moved budget season forward four months just to understand his AI tool costs, and treats the output like a junior analyst he triple-checks. Bodycote’s board ran a three-month process and landed 940p from Veritas rather than taking the first approach from Apollo or CVC. Axcel spent this fundraise widening its LP base into Asia, which pays off in the fund after next, not this one. David Shapiro’s column says the investments are fine, your behaviour is the problem.
