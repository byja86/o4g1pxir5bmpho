Theme of the day: Build it, or buy it — and what each choice really costs. Five of today’s eight items are the same decision seen from different chairs. Two private equity consortia are circling GFL Environmental, a Canadian waste group with roughly $19bn of equity and $10bn of debt, in what would be one of the year’s largest leveraged buyouts — buying control at scale, with the founder rolling his entire stake in rather than cashing out. KKR has just passed Partners Group as Europe’s largest evergreen manager on €13.6bn, having built a private-wealth channel from a standing start in 2023 while its Swiss rival gates redemptions on a $14.5bn open-ended fund — a clean lesson in what an open-ended structure costs you when investors all want out at once. In Johannesburg, a 181-year-old insurer is about to sign its millionth banking customer having built the bank itself, and Outsurance is compounding on a patience playbook that says build the book slowly in a new market rather than buy distribution. Meanwhile Wayve has hired the CFO who took Waymo from pre-revenue to five cities, which is the hiring version of the same question: buy the experience, or grow it. Today’s CPD is an ACCA M&A course — financial due diligence and valuation, 7.0 verifiable units with a certificate — and it is the most directly on-thesis thing for a future sponsor that the finder has produced, with one honest caveat about pricing set out below.
